# Bigbowl Corporate Utilities Website — Brief Plan

## Goal
Create an attractive, official React website for Bigbowl that helps companies explore corporate gifts and office utilities, view product details, and contact the team for enquiries.

## Brand direction
Use the supplied visiting card as the visual reference: bright yellow and white surfaces, deep red accents, bold clean typography, and the Bigbowl logo. Keep the layout polished, trustworthy, mobile-friendly, and product photography prominent.

## Website structure
- **Home:** Brand introduction, featured products, category highlights, customer/brand credibility, and clear enquiry calls to action.
- **Categories:** Onboarding Gifts, Festival Gifts, and Office Utilities, each with browsable product listings.
- **Product details:** Product name, images, price, concise description, and an enquiry action.
- **Contact:** Phone and business address, plus an enquiry form.

## Product management and backend
- Build the customer-facing site with React.
- Use Supabase for product data and image storage.
- Create a protected back-office area to add, edit, publish, and remove products; assign categories, prices, descriptions, and images.
- Feature selected products on the homepage for marketing; load published products from Supabase.
